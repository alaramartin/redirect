# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 11, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the "Redirect" Chrome extension (the "Extension").

## What data the Extension collects

The Extension tracks, for your own use only:

- **Website usage** — the hostname (and, for path-scoped limits, the URL path) of sites you visit, and how much time you spend on each, per day.
- **Your configuration** — the limits, categories, redirect targets, and focus-mode settings you create within the Extension.

The Extension does not collect your name, email address, age, or any other personally identifiable information. It does not access health, financial, authentication, or personal communication data. It does not read the content of the pages you visit (text, images, video) — only the URL and time spent. It does not log clicks, keystrokes, mouse movement, or scrolling.

## How this data is stored and used

All data listed above is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in `storage` API (`chrome.storage.local`). It is used exclusively to power the Extension's own features: enforcing the time limits and category budgets you set, showing your usage analytics, and performing redirects when a limit is reached.

## What we do not do

- We do not transmit any of your data off of your device. There is no server this data is sent to.
- We do not sell or share your data with any third party.
- We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose unrelated to the Extension's screen-time features described above.
- We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

## Data removal

Uninstalling the Extension removes all locally stored data. You can also clear individual limits or reset all limits from within the Extension's popup at any time.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a revised effective date.
